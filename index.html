🎯 Targeted Pronunciation Drills

1. S vs SH (Hiss vs Hush)

👄 Mouth Placement: For 'S', keep tongue behind teeth. For 'SH', pull tongue back and round your lips like you are saying "Shhh!"

2. Z vs J (The Bee Buzz vs The Jump)

👄 Mouth Placement: 'Z' is a vibration. Touch your throat—it should shake! 'J' is a quick stop/burst of air.

3. O (Closed) vs AU (Open/Logic)

👄 Mouth Placement: 'O' (Ore) uses a small circle. 'AU' (Logic) requires you to drop your jaw much lower.
